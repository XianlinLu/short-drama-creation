# Story Example Fixture: Mountain Relay Station

This is an acceptance fixture, not reusable prose.

A courier reaches an abandoned mountain relay station carrying a sealed message addressed to someone who died years earlier. The test story should establish the physical situation immediately, give the courier a concrete reason to deliver the message before dawn, and escalate through altered evidence rather than repeated attacks.

Expected craft: one object-evidence engine, one deadline, dialogue with concealed leverage, a location that restricts choices, and an ending in which delivery changes the meaning of the recipient's reported death.

The result passes only if characters, causal chain, wording, imagery, and resolution are newly created.
