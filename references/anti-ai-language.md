# Natural Language Texture

Use this check when prose feels generic or mechanically patterned. Prefer viewpoint-specific observations, concrete verbs, uneven but purposeful sentence rhythm, and emotions demonstrated through choices or physical behavior.

Repair repeated contrast formulas, symmetrical slogans, stacked abstractions, redundant conclusions, and ornate sentences that hide the literal event. Apply the diagnosis by function in the story language rather than searching for one fixed phrase.

The opening must be understandable at the literal level while preserving a meaningful unanswered question. Dialogue may be stylized when it fits a character, but narration should not depend on reusable templates.
