# Originality And Quality Review

## Originality boundary

Create new narrative expression. Reference material may inform broad functions such as delayed revelation, escalating trials, institutional pressure, romantic push-pull, or visual contrast. It must not supply copied wording, recognizable characters, branded settings, signature objects, iconic staging, or a distinctive scene sequence.

For a living creator style request, translate the request into neutral craft settings: sentence length, point-of-view distance, dialogue density, chronology, humor level, sensory focus, or reveal frequency. Never promise direct imitation.

## Narrative review

Score each relevant item as pass, repair, or not applicable:

1. **Immediate orientation:** the first paragraphs establish who, where, and what changed.
2. **Desire:** the protagonist pursues an observable goal.
3. **Pressure:** opposition has a specific lever and motive.
4. **Escalation:** later complications alter risk or available choices.
5. **Causality:** the climax grows from prior choices, rules, or evidence.
6. **Dialogue:** speech applies pressure or reveals a relationship instead of explaining the plot.
7. **Image function:** objects and settings participate in the story.
8. **Ending:** the final beat creates consequence, choice, or reinterpretation.
9. **Genre promise:** the expected emotional experience is delivered.
10. **Completeness:** the requested form ends rather than stopping at setup.

Repair failed items before delivery when the requested task includes a finished story.

## Language texture

Prefer specific verbs, varied sentence movement, and details chosen by viewpoint. Remove generic emotional labels when action, body response, dialogue, or object interaction can carry the feeling.

Watch for repeated machine-like templates, including excessive “not X but Y” narration, stacked abstract nouns, symmetrical three-part claims, redundant conclusions, and ornate openings that obscure literal events. A character may use a patterned phrase when it fits their voice, but narration should not depend on it.

Apply the same diagnostic by function in every language rather than searching only for one Chinese or English phrase.

## Visual continuity review

For media output, compare each returned artifact with the continuity record:

- face, silhouette, proportions, hair, clothing layers, palette, accessories;
- environment geometry, prop location, light direction, weather, and time of day;
- action direction, camera orientation, emotional state, and story consequence;
- resolution, aspect ratio, frame rate, and audio policy.

Small intentional changes are acceptable when explained by the story. Unexplained identity or spatial drift requires repair of the failed generation step.

## Safety

Do not create sexual content involving minors, instructional violence, or content that celebrates coercive criminal abuse. Avoid assigning crimes or scandals to real private people. For risky story material, fictionalize identities, reduce operational detail, and focus on consequence and emotion.
