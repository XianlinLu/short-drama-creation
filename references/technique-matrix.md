# Craft Controls

Translate a requested effect into adjustable craft choices:

- Opening: anomaly, irreversible action, broken expectation, or urgent decision.
- Viewpoint: close emotional access, observational distance, or controlled information gap.
- Pacing: scene length, reveal interval, dialogue density, and transition compression.
- Conflict: direct opposition, procedural restriction, relationship leverage, or moral tradeoff.
- Dialogue: bargaining, evasion, status shift, accusation, or withheld answer.
- Imagery: recurring object, spatial barrier, changing light, or physical trace.
- Reversal: evidence reclassification, allegiance shift, cost disclosure, or rule reinterpretation.
- Ending: consequence, transformed relationship, unresolved larger threat, or image with changed meaning.

Select controls for the chosen genre and audience. Do not equate craft settings with copying a creator's recognizable style.
