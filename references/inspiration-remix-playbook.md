# Reference Recombination

Treat familiar stories and creator references as prompts for abstract craft analysis. Extract functions such as delayed recognition, public proof, restricted access, reversal timing, relationship pressure, investigation structure, or visual rhythm.

Recombine selected functions with new characters, setting, stakes, causal logic, scenes, dialogue, props, and images. Do not copy recognizable expression or imitate a living creator's distinctive voice.

When several new combinations are worth offering, form exactly four direction bundles and send them only through the native selector described in `references/interaction-and-topic-ui.md`.
