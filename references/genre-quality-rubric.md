# Genre Promise Checks

Use only the checks relevant to the selected direction.

- Suspense: clues precede the solution and each answer changes the investigation.
- Romance: attraction and resistance both affect decisions; vulnerability has a cost.
- Comeback or revenge: evidence and timing earn the reversal; victory creates consequence.
- Fantasy or progression: rules appear through pressure; gains change strategy and have limits.
- Science fiction: one speculative condition shapes ordinary behavior and the climax.
- Workplace or institutional satire: factions have concrete incentives, fears, and procedural leverage.
- Children's story: vocabulary and threat fit the audience; wonder is carried by action and image.

Across genres, require an observable desire, changing conflict, causal climax, and ending consequence. Do not replace story movement with explanation.
